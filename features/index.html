FEATURES

Container trucking software features, and where each one stands.

PortIntermodal is intermodal and drayage TMS software for container trucking companies. Every feature carries its real status. In Development means it exists in the product and is not yet generally released.

Status key: Planned In Development Available for Demo

Dispatch and Operations

In Development

Plan moves, assign people and equipment, and catch problems before the truck leaves the yard.

  • Orders, jobs and multi-move routes with ordered stops and appointments
  • Dispatch board with driver, truck and chassis assignment
  • Conflict checks for schedule overlaps, licence, hazmat, weight and permits
  • Driver suggestions ranked clear, caution or blocked, using ELD hours and truck position
  • Locations directory with geocoding and company-centred maps

Job Tracking and Live Maps

In Development

See what is happening on each job without calling the driver.

  • Planned route and live truck position on a map
  • Stop events and container custody history
  • Order email notifications

Driver App

In Development

A responsive web app that installs as a PWA, so drivers need no app store.

  • Driver login tied to a driver record, with accept or decline of loads
  • Arrive and depart stop events, and map directions
  • Load board: take a load, ask for one, hand one back
  • Problem reports to dispatch
  • Inbox with a bell and unread count
  • Offline queue that saves work without signal and sends it later, encrypted on the phone
  • Web push notifications (written, not yet verified on real devices)

Paperless Documentation

In Development

Replace paper gate tickets and delivery receipts with captured, verifiable records.

  • Camera capture of gate tickets, EIRs and receipts, with compression, retry and PDF support
  • Receiver signature and server-timed proof of delivery as a PDF, with a tamper-evident hash chain
  • Equipment checks of container, chassis and seal against the job
  • Values read off documents, flagged when they differ from the job and never written over it

Containers and Visibility

In Development

Know where a container is and when it costs you money.

  • Port and terminal container visibility with auto-tracking
  • Container matching
  • Last free day
  • Keyword and semantic search

Billing and Invoicing

In Development

From completed move to invoice and settlement.

  • Invoices and customer rates
  • Driver and owner-operator settlements
  • Payables
  • Invoice and statement PDFs

Odoo Accounting Integration

In Development

An optional accounting layer. Dispatch and operations never depend on it.

  • Every tenant gets its own Odoo database, provisioned automatically from a chart-of-accounts template, or can bring their own Odoo
  • Single sign-on through Keycloak
  • Invoices sync to Odoo, payments sync back, driver and vendor bills sync as payables
  • Tax codes managed by each company, with Odoo doing the tax calculation
  • Background drift check that flags differences between PortIntermodal and Odoo
  • Background sync with retries
  • Aging, books and accounting views
  • Odoo database snapshots and encrypted dumps to object storage

AI Assistant and MCP Server

In Development

AI that uses the same permissioned actions as the app.

  • In-app assistant, switched on per company; each company picks its AI provider and supplies its own key, stored encrypted
  • Task-focused skills with their own role limits, such as investigating a container, which a driver can do without browsing company data
  • Tools that call the same backend actions as the app, so tenant isolation and permissions still apply
  • Reads shipments, containers, chassis, drivers, equipment checks, rates, exceptions and port visibility, plus driver suggestions and last free day
  • Meaning-based semantic search over shipment notes and statuses, alongside keyword search
  • Read-only MCP server so outside AI agents such as Claude can query PortIntermodal
  • Written agent guide and playbooks for browser agents, such as entering orders from documents

Platform and Security

In Development

Built for several companies on one system without mixing their data.

  • Multi-tenant with company isolation (row-level security, and a separate Odoo database)
  • Roles: admin, dispatcher, accounting, viewer and driver
  • Keycloak sign-in
  • ELD integration (Samsara)
  • Reports and dashboard
