FEATURES

What PortIntermodal will do, and where each feature stands.

Every feature is labeled with its actual status. Planned means it has not been built. In Development means it exists in the prototype and is not finished.

Status key: Planned In Development Available for Demo

Dispatch Management

In Development

Assign drivers and equipment to moves, and keep the dispatch board in step with what is happening on the street.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Dispatch board with move assignment
  • Driver and chassis availability at a glance
  • Pickup, delivery, and return sequencing
  • Exception and delay flags

Container Management

In Development

One record per container, from booking through empty return, including per diem and demurrage exposure.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Container and chassis records
  • Last free day and return-by dates
  • Per diem and demurrage tracking
  • Booking and bill of lading references

Driver Management

In Development

Coordinate drivers, capture proof of delivery, and keep qualification and pay information in one place.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Driver profiles and compliance documents
  • Mobile-friendly assignments and status updates
  • Proof of delivery capture
  • Driver settlement inputs

Shipment Tracking

Planned

A single timeline of milestones for each shipment so staff and customers see the same status.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Shipment milestone timeline
  • Status updates from dispatch activity
  • Event history and audit trail

Terminal Operations

Planned

Track terminal and depot information that affects when and where a container can move.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Terminal and depot directory
  • Appointment tracking
  • Availability and hold visibility

Customer Portal

Planned

Give shippers a read-oriented view of their shipments and documents without calling the office.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Shipment status lookup
  • Document access
  • Quote and delivery request intake

Billing and Invoicing

In Development

Turn completed moves and accessorials into invoices without re-keying data.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Rate and accessorial setup
  • Invoice generation from completed moves
  • Invoice status tracking

Reporting

In Development

Operational and financial reports built from the same records dispatch and billing use.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Operational summaries
  • Revenue and cost reporting
  • Exportable data

Orders and Customers

In Development

Capture orders and keep customer records that feed dispatch, containers, and billing.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Order entry
  • Customer records
  • Orders linked to containers and moves

Trucks and Equipment

In Development

Track the trucks and equipment that moves are assigned to.

PLANNED SCOPE

  • Truck records
  • Equipment records
  • Availability for assignment
