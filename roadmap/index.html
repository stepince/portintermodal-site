ROADMAP

Six phases from foundation to integrations.

Phases are listed in planned order. A prototype already covers parts of several phases. Dates appear only when they have been confirmed.

  1. 1

    Phase 1: Foundation and Architecture

    Establish the technical foundation and core domain model.

    • Architecture and domain model
    • Application scaffolding and environments
    • Data model for customers, orders, and containers
  2. 2

    Phase 2: Orders and Containers

    Capture orders and manage containers through their lifecycle.

    • Order entry
    • Container and chassis records
    • Last free day and per diem tracking
  3. 3

    Phase 3: Dispatch Management

    Plan and assign moves.

    • Dispatch board
    • Move assignment
    • Terminal and depot information
  4. 4

    Phase 4: Driver Operations

    Connect drivers to their assignments.

    • Driver profiles and documents
    • Mobile-friendly driver workflow
    • Proof of delivery
  5. 5

    Phase 5: Billing and Customer Portal

    Invoice completed work and give customers visibility.

    • Rates and accessorials
    • Invoicing
    • Customer shipment visibility
  6. 6

    Phase 6: Integrations and Reporting

    Connect to outside systems and report on operations.

    • Integrations with external systems
    • Operational and financial reporting
    • Data export

Phase 6 integrations are designed around customer-managed visibility provider accounts. See how this helps reduce software costs →
