Drayage runs on tight windows and scattered data.

PortIntermodal is being designed around the day-to-day problems of container trucking operations.

Disconnected tools

Dispatch, container data, driver communication, and billing often live in separate systems.

Time-sensitive containers

Last free day, per diem, and demurrage deadlines are easy to miss when tracked by hand.

Re-keyed data

The same order is typed into multiple places, from dispatch through to the invoice.

Limited visibility

Customers call the office for status that the system should already show.

Planned platform capabilities

Each capability shows its real status. Nothing here is available yet unless its label says so.

Dispatch Management

In Development

Assign drivers and equipment to moves, and keep the dispatch board in step with what is happening on the street.

Container Management

In Development

One record per container, from booking through empty return, including per diem and demurrage exposure.

Driver Management

In Development

Coordinate drivers, capture proof of delivery, and keep qualification and pay information in one place.

Shipment Tracking

Planned

A single timeline of milestones for each shipment so staff and customers see the same status.

Terminal Operations

Planned

Track terminal and depot information that affects when and where a container can move.

Customer Portal
