PLATFORM

One connected record for every container move.

PortIntermodal is being built so that the order, the container, the dispatch, the driver, and the invoice share the same data.

Integrated by design, not by integration.

Many operations stitch together separate tools for dispatch, tracking, and billing. The goal is a single platform where each stage feeds the next. This describes planned functionality; see the features page for the status of each area.

Dispatch management

Plan and assign moves against real container and driver data.

Container tracking

Follow each container and its deadlines from booking to empty return.

Driver operations

Send assignments to drivers and receive status and proof of delivery.

Terminal coordination

Keep terminal and depot information next to the moves that depend on it.

Customer visibility

Let customers see shipment status without a phone call.

Billing and invoicing

Invoice from completed moves instead of re-entering them.
