Why Intermodal Trucking Software Gets Expensive

Software spending in a drayage operation rarely comes from one line item. It builds up from several places:

  • A TMS subscription, often priced per user, per truck or per load.
  • Container tracking and visibility services, billed separately or bundled into a larger plan.
  • Add-on modules for billing, driver apps or reporting.
  • Time spent re-keying data between systems and chasing container status by hand.

The result is that many small and midsize trucking companies pay for coverage and features they only partly use. Reviewing each piece separately is the first step toward controlling the total.

Avoid Paying for Unnecessary Visibility Subscriptions

Container visibility services are priced by provider, plan and volume. If your company already has a relationship with a visibility provider that covers the terminals and routes you run, buying a second bundle of tracking through your TMS can mean paying twice for similar coverage.

PortIntermodal is designed so that visibility is not tied to a bundle. You decide which providers you need, and you keep the agreement and pricing you negotiated with them. This does not remove provider fees. Those remain between you and the provider. It gives you the choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my existing container visibility provider?

PortIntermodal supports integrations with selected providers. Compatibility depends on the provider and available API access. Today the first adapter, for Terminal49, is built and in development; other providers are planned or under evaluation. Ask us about yours.

Do I need to purchase container visibility through PortIntermodal?

PortIntermodal is designed to support customer-managed provider accounts rather than requiring all visibility services to be purchased through the TMS.

Can I use multiple visibility providers?

The architecture supports multiple providers, allowing companies to combine coverage where supported. Each supported provider is added as its own integration, so coverage grows as integrations are completed.

Who pays the visibility provider fees?

Customers manage their own agreements and fees with third-party visibility providers. Bringing your own account does not remove those fees; it lets you choose and control them.

Will PortIntermodal help configure integrations?

Yes. Assisted onboarding helps customers connect supported providers and validate their setup. Onboarding is assisted rather than fully self-service for now.

Can PortIntermodal help reduce dispatcher workload?

PortIntermodal is designed to reduce repetitive manual work through centralized tracking, workflow automation and operational visibility. Parts of the platform are still in development, so ask for a demo to see what is available today.
